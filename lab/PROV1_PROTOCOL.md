# PROV-1 protocol: can the being use an explicit record of its own experience to know what it doesn't know? (design draft v0 for Sol's review; 2026-11-03)

- **Basis:** SUPPORT-1 closeout and decision (lab/SUPPORT1_PROTOCOL.md, f821e48): cortex-only UNKNOWN training
  transferred strongly to untrained gaps of negatively trained identities but inadequately to held-out experienced
  identities, and that transfer decayed across sleeps. Decision (Claude, Sol concurring): give the being an explicit,
  inspectable support/provenance record, as an engineering choice, not a claim that weights cannot represent absence.
- **This draft is design-level.** The implementation spec (exact RNG keys, file schemas, launcher) follows after design
  GO, the same way SUPPORT-1 went from NEXT drafts to the frozen protocol. Anything not stated here is as SUPPORT-1.
- Development experiment. Nothing here authorises spending; David decides and launches.

## 1. Question, claim boundary, and the place of this test in the being
- **Question.** When the being answers with a record of *what it experienced about whom* in hand (not the values),
  does it
  1. still answer supported cells correctly;
  2. abstain on unsupported cells, **including for identities never used in any negative training** (the H people,
     where SUPPORT-1 failed) and unfamiliar names;
  3. actually follow the record (behaviour changes when the record is wrong or absent); and
  4. do so through learned use at least as well as a simple rule that gates on the record?
- **Two separable contributions (Sol):**
  - *value of the information:* the same checkpoint under FAITHFUL vs ABSENT vs corrupted records;
  - *ability to use it:* zero-shot use (record shown at test only) vs trained use vs the gate rule.
- **Claim boundary.**
  - The record is generated by an oracle from the checkpoint's true history: perfect fidelity, and acknowledged as
    **additional information** the cortex-only arms of SUPPORT-1 did not have. Success shows the being can *use* such a
    record, not that its own episodic store can *produce* one.
  - The record carries **support only** (which attributes were experienced, and in which life), never values, so
    every correct value still has to come from the consolidated cortex. A values-bearing record would turn the test
    into retrieval; it is reported only as a reference (FULL, phase A).
  - UNKNOWN keeps SUPPORT-1's meaning: not supported by the experiences acquired by this checkpoint.
- **Where it leads.** PROV-2 replaces the oracle record with one produced by the being's own episodic store
  (being/cls/episodic.py: per-episode adapters with provenance, routed with a null-memory threshold), so record
  fidelity becomes a measured quantity rather than a given.

## 2. Streams, strata, probes
- Exactly SUPPORT-1's seeds (20–23 development; 24 calibration), streams, fork record, strata (PM, SEL/HOW split, REV,
  HNM, FRESH), probe inventories, QDIAG 64, oracle labels, normalisation and grading. Reusing them lets phase A run on
  the SUPPORT-1 checkpoints and makes every PROV-1 number directly comparable with SUPPORT-1's.
- **Held-out identities are the primary test population:** H entities' PM cells (HNM), FRESH names, and H positives.
  No arm ever receives a negative (UNKNOWN-target) item about an H entity or a FRESH name.

## 3. The support record
- **Content at checkpoint L for queried entity e:** the set of core attributes a for which the history has a fact
  (e, a) with life ≤ L, each with the life of its latest fact, in ATTRS9 order. Values never appear.
- **Rendering (one fixed format, on its own line immediately before the probe line, Question or Statement, inside
  the same FRAME):**
  ```
  Memory record: {e} - experienced: workplace (life 1), hobby (life 3). Nothing else was experienced about {e}.
  ```
  and for an entity with no experiences: `Memory record: {e} - no experiences.`
  - One wording only (no paraphrase of the record), so the record cannot be the source of a construction effect.
  - Leak check: the record must not contain any value token of e (verified at prep; STOP otherwise).
  - Length: the full sequence stays ≤ 256 tokens (STOP otherwise).
- **Evaluation conditions** (per probe, the record is about the probed entity):
  - **FAITHFUL:** as above, from the history the checkpoint lived (observed, or withheld for fork branches).
  - **ABSENT:** no record line (SUPPORT-1's frame exactly).
  - **OMIT:** FAITHFUL with the probed attribute removed. Probed on supported cells only. The record-following answer
    is UNKNOWN. Primary reliance measure.
  - **ADD:** FAITHFUL with the probed (unsupported) attribute inserted at its true ATTRS9 position, life = L. Probed
    on unsupported cells only. Reported descriptively: with no value in the cortex, both an attempt and an abstention
    are defensible (a "know that I learned it but can't recall" state), so ADD has no pass threshold.
  - **FULL** (phase A reference only): FAITHFUL plus values.
- **Gate baseline (GATE):** take the checkpoint's ABSENT answer; if the FAITHFUL record lacks the probed attribute,
  replace it with UNKNOWN. A rule, computed offline from existing answers; it costs no GPU time.

## 4. Phases and arms
### Phase A: zero-shot use on SUPPORT-1 cortexes (eval only)
- Checkpoints: SUPPORT-1 arms **P** and **U4**, sleeps 1–5, seeds 20–23 (40 checkpoints), plus fork branches.
- Conditions: FAITHFUL, OMIT, ADD, FULL. ABSENT is SUPPORT-1's existing evaluation, reused byte for byte.
- **Checkpoints are regenerated on the pod, not uploaded:** run SUPPORT-1's frozen train stage (CODE_HASH 38b8e705…)
  and compare every checkpoint's sha256 with results/support1's support1_ckpt.sha256 (108 entries, on David's E:
  drive and on record). Proceed only on an exact match for all 40 used; a mismatch is a STOP (the fallback is an
  upload from E:, decided then). This also tests SUPPORT-1's end-to-end reproducibility.
- Merged evaluation only; one merged/unmerged twin per seed as an integrity spot check. No competence monitors (no
  weights change in phase A).
- Purpose: if zero-shot FAITHFUL use already meets §5 on held-out identities, the being needs the record but no new
  training for this capability, and phase B tests whether training adds robustness. Either way phase B runs unless
  phase A shows the record is unusable even with training in prospect (STOP condition below).

### Phase B: trained use (record in the sleep curriculum)
- Same consolidation pipeline, budget, E = 4, LoRA settings and five sleeps as SUPPORT-1's U4 arm, with the
  FAITHFUL record in the frame of every training Question item. Arms:
  - **RP:** positives only, with records (controls for the frame change; does the record alone induce abstention?).
  - **RU:** RP + SUPPORT-1's U4 negative block (A entities' SEL cells only, UNKNOWN targets), with records.
  - **RC:** RU + counterfactual record items: supported A cells shown with an OMIT record and target UNKNOWN, at a fixed
    fraction of the negative block (proposal: one quarter), budget-matched by shortening RU's negative block. This
    teaches that the record is authoritative for "did I experience this".
  - **N** (base, no training) for denominators and FRESH reference, as in SUPPORT-1.
- Budget matching: every arm has the same number of optimiser items per sleep as SUPPORT-1's U4 (positives filled to
  budget for RP), so arms differ in content, not amount.
- Evaluation: all §3 conditions except FULL, merged + unmerged twins as in SUPPORT-1, competence monitors on.
- Fork: observed vs withheld history for RU and RC at sleep 4, as in SUPPORT-1, with the record following the
  branch's own history.

## 5. Criteria (pre-registered)
A configuration ("being") is an arm × record condition. The **candidate being** is FAITHFUL with the zero-shot P
cortex (phase A) or with any trained arm (phase B). Thresholds are ABST-0/SUPPORT-1's where an analogue exists.
- **K1 acquisition (D1 analogue):** POS paraphrase correct ≥ P(ABSENT) − 0.10 at sleeps 2–5, and FIRST ≥ 0.50. P is
  SUPPORT-1's P arm (same seeds and streams), so phase B needs no new P training.
- **K2 held-out abstention (the SUPPORT-1 failure):** HNM UNKNOWN ≥ 0.90 at **every** sleep 2–5, pooled, and ≥ 0.80 in
  each seed; FRESH UNKNOWN ≥ 0.90 at every sleep.
- **K3 trained-identity abstention:** SEL-D and HOW UNKNOWN ≥ 0.90 at sleeps 2–5 (for zero-shot P, which never saw a
  negative, these are held-out cells too).
- **K4 retention and competence (D4 analogue):** RET ≥ P − 0.10 at sleep 5; competence ≤ 1.02 pooled and ≤ 1.05 per
  seed (phase B only).
- **K5 reliance:** under OMIT, UNKNOWN on supported cells ≥ 0.80 at sleeps 2–5, pooled, including H positives.
- **K6 history:** the fork is history-sensitive (observed correct and withheld UNKNOWN on the H transition cells) in
  ≥ 3/4 seeds (phase B arms with a fork; reported for phase A).
- **K7 learned vs rule:** on the composite of K1–K3 cells the candidate is within 0.05 of GATE (reported with GATE's
  own values; GATE is not a candidate, since it is a rule written for the being).
- **Verdicts:**
  - `PROV1_ADVANCE`: a candidate meets K1–K7. Reported as ZERO_SHOT (phase A) or TRAINED (arm named).
  - `PROV1_GATE_ONLY`: no candidate meets K1–K7, but GATE meets K1–K4. The record carries the needed information; the
    learned system does not use it well enough.
  - `PROV1_NO_ADVANCE`: neither.
- **Mechanism readings (descriptive):** FAITHFUL − ABSENT per stratum (value of information); OMIT/ADD effects
  (reliance); RU − RP and RC − RU (does training to use the record matter; does counterfactual training produce
  reliance); per-sleep trajectories of HNM UNKNOWN (does SUPPORT-1's decay disappear when the record is present?).
- **Phase-A STOP:** if FAITHFUL improves HNM UNKNOWN over ABSENT by < 0.10 for both P and U4 at every sleep, AND
  OMIT reliance is < 0.20, phase B is reviewed again before spending (the record format may be unreadable).

## 6. Predictions (Claude, before any run)
- Phase A: FAITHFUL raises HNM UNKNOWN substantially for zero-shot P (to roughly 0.5–0.8) but misses K2; OMIT reliance
  is partial (0.3–0.6); U4 with records is better on HNM than without but still decays with sleep.
- Phase B: RU and RC meet K2 and K3; RC meets K5, RU is uncertain on K5; RP shows little abstention. K7 met by RC.
  Most likely verdict: `PROV1_ADVANCE` (TRAINED, RC). Least certain: K1 at sleep 2, given SUPPORT-1's early
  acquisition cost.

## 7. Cost and order
- Phase A: regenerate checkpoints (about 10 min per seed) + about 40 × 4 evaluations without competence monitors.
  Measured by a phase-0 timing on the calibration seed before launch; expected a few dollars.
- Phase B: 4 arms, same scale as SUPPORT-1 phase 2 (about $12–15 at $0.99/h).
- Proposed ceiling for PROV-1: $30. Order: implementation → Sol review → phase 0 (prep + validation, no GPU) →
  phase A (GO from both) → phase B (GO from both).
- **Disk:** checkpoints are written to David's E: drive (3.9 TB free) and streamed with verification; no transient
  archive copy on C:.

## 8. Checks against David's questions
- **Primary goal:** a persistent individual that knows what it knows; SUPPORT-1 showed the cortex alone does not
  learn that for people it was not drilled on. This tests the missing structure (an explicit experience record, the
  analogue of hippocampal source memory) before building it into the being.
- **What would change direction:** if neither zero-shot nor trained use reaches K2/K5, an explicit record is not
  sufficient either, and the next question is the frame/curriculum, not the record. If only GATE succeeds, the being
  should keep the record and the open problem is learned use. A fresh guard-passing cortex-only result with held-out
  abstention would reopen the weights-only route.

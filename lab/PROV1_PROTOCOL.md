# PROV-1 protocol: can the being use an explicit record of its own experience to know what it doesn't know? (design v1, Sol GO at design level; 2026-11-03)

- **Basis:** SUPPORT-1 closeout and decision (lab/SUPPORT1_PROTOCOL.md, f821e48): cortex-only UNKNOWN training
  transferred strongly to untrained gaps of negatively trained identities but inadequately to held-out experienced
  identities, and that transfer decayed across sleeps. Decision (Claude, Sol concurring): give the being an explicit,
  inspectable support/provenance record, as an engineering choice, not a claim that weights cannot represent absence.
- **v1** answers Sol's design HOLD on v0 (97dcae0). Each change is tagged [H1]–[H12]; the list is in §10.
- **Design-level.** The implementation spec (exact RNG keys, file schemas, launcher) follows after design GO, as
  SUPPORT-1 did. Anything not stated here is as SUPPORT-1.
- Development experiment. Nothing here authorises spending; David decides and launches.

## 1. Question, claim boundary, place in the being
- **Question.** When the being answers with a record of *what it experienced about whom* in hand (not the values),
  does it
  1. still answer supported cells correctly, for trained and held-out identities alike;
  2. abstain on unsupported cells, **including for identities never used in any negative training** (the H people,
     where SUPPORT-1 failed) and unfamiliar names, at every sleep;
  3. follow the record specifically: deleting the queried entry changes the answer, deleting a different entry
     does not; and
  4. reach the reliability of a fixed gate rule through learned use?
- **Two separable contributions (Sol):** *value of the information* (the same checkpoint under FAITHFUL vs ABSENT vs
  corrupted records) and *ability to use it* (zero-shot vs trained use vs the gate).
- **Claim boundary.**
  - The record is generated by an oracle from the checkpoint's true history: perfect fidelity, acknowledged as
    **additional information** the cortex-only arms of SUPPORT-1 did not have. Success shows the being can *use* such
    a record, not that its episodic store can *produce* one.
  - The record carries **support only** (attribute and life, never values); every correct value comes from the
    consolidated cortex. FULL (with values) is a phase-A reference only.
  - Following the record as an authoritative ledger ("answer only what is listed") is the **intended** behaviour, not
    a shortcut. It is not a claim that the being independently judges whether a value is available to it (Sol).
  - UNKNOWN keeps SUPPORT-1's meaning: not supported by the experiences acquired by this checkpoint.
  - GATE_ONLY would be an implementation outcome (the learned system does not use a perfect ledger well enough), not a
    discovery that the ledger carries support information, since the oracle record guarantees that [H8].
- **Where it leads.** PROV-2 replaces the oracle record with one produced by the being's own episodic store
  (being/cls/episodic.py: per-episode adapters with provenance, routed with a null-memory threshold), so record
  fidelity becomes measured rather than given.

## 2. Streams, strata, probes
- Exactly SUPPORT-1's seeds (20–23 development; 24 calibration), streams, fork record, strata (PM, SEL/HOW split, REV,
  HNM, FRESH), probe inventories, QDIAG 64, oracle labels, normalisation and grading, so phase A runs on SUPPORT-1's
  checkpoints and all numbers are directly comparable.
- **Held-out population:** H entities (positives and PM cells), FRESH names. No arm ever receives a negative,
  counterfactual or any UNKNOWN-target item about an H entity or a FRESH name.
- **Disjoint cohorts used by the criteria [H9]:** POS-A, POS-H (positive probes on A / H entities, current facts),
  RET (retention probes), SEL-D (delivered SEL cells), HOW, HNM, FRESH, REV. A probe belongs to exactly one cohort,
  as in SUPPORT-1's prep.

## 3. The support record
- **Content at checkpoint L for queried entity e, under history h:** the core attributes a with a fact (e, a) in h at
  life ≤ L, each with the life of its latest such fact, in ATTRS9 order. Never values.
- **Rendering:** one fixed line immediately before the probe line (Question or Statement), inside the same FRAME:
  ```
  Memory record: {e} - experienced: workplace (life 1), hobby (life 3). Nothing else was experienced about {e}.
  ```
  and `Memory record: {e} - no experiences.` when the set is empty.
  - One wording only. No condition name (FAITHFUL, OMIT, …) ever appears in any prompt [H4].
  - Prep STOPs if a record contains any value token of e, or a sequence exceeds 256 tokens.
- **Evaluation conditions** (the record is about the probed entity):
  - **FAITHFUL:** from the history the checkpoint lived (observed, or the withheld branch's own history for forks).
  - **ABSENT:** no record line (SUPPORT-1's frame exactly).
  - **DEL-Q (queried deletion) [H4]:** FAITHFUL minus the probed attribute. On supported cells.
  - **DEL-X (irrelevant deletion) [H4]:** FAITHFUL minus one *other* experienced attribute of e (chosen by a keyed
    draw among e's other entries). On supported cells of entities with ≥ 2 experienced attributes.
  - **ADD:** FAITHFUL plus the probed (unsupported) attribute at its ATTRS9 position, life = L. On unsupported
    cells. Descriptive only.
  - **FULL** (phase A reference only): FAITHFUL plus values.
- **Two labels on every row [H5].**
  - *Truth label:* the oracle on the history actually lived. Corruption never changes it: a DEL-Q row on a supported
    cell still has the true value as its truth label, and an ADD row's truth label stays UNKNOWN, so an invented
    answer under ADD is a confabulation even though the corrupted record "authorised" answering.
  - *Compliance target:* what the shown record implies: UNKNOWN when the probed attribute is absent from the shown
    record, otherwise the truth label. Undefined under ADD (not graded for compliance).
  - FAITHFUL criteria use the truth label (identical to compliance under FAITHFUL). K5 uses the paired
    interventions below.
- **Gate baselines [H7].**
  - **P-GATE (main comparator):** frozen SUPPORT-1 P's ABSENT answers, replaced by UNKNOWN wherever the FAITHFUL record
    lacks the probed attribute. Offline; no GPU.
  - **Own-gate (diagnostic):** the same rule on each candidate's own ABSENT answers. Reported only, because ABSENT is
    an unfamiliar prompt for record-trained models.

## 4. Phases and arms
### Phase A: zero-shot use on SUPPORT-1 cortexes (eval only; diagnostic, not ADVANCE-eligible) [H6]
- **Inventory [H11]:** SUPPORT-1 P and U4, sleeps 1–5, seeds 20–23 (40 checkpoints) plus the 4 U4 sleep-4 withheld
  checkpoints: 44 in all.
- **Regeneration with bound identities [H11]:** the pod reruns SUPPORT-1's frozen train stage (CODE_HASH 38b8e705…)
  and checks each of the 44 against the committed SUPPORT-1 train logs (`ckpt_sha256`, and `full_state_hash`, which
  covers tensors, RNG state and step). Admitted if `ckpt_sha256` matches; if only the file hash differs and
  `full_state_hash` matches, the tensors are equal and the checkpoint is admitted with a note; otherwise STOP reuse
  pending investigation or a verified upload from David's E: drive (108 sha256-verified copies).
- Conditions: FAITHFUL, DEL-Q, DEL-X, ADD, FULL. ABSENT is SUPPORT-1's archived evaluation, reused byte for byte.
- **Integrity [H10]:** merged and unmerged twins for every evaluation that feeds a criterion or reading, with
  SUPPORT-1's twin rules (0 answer/outcome mismatches). No competence monitors (no weights change).
- **Phase-A outcome:** `PROV1A_ZERO_SHOT_PASS` if a configuration (P or U4, FAITHFUL) meets K1–K5 and K7; the U4 fork
  is reported for K6 descriptively; otherwise `PROV1A_ZERO_SHOT_FAIL`. Either way it is a review point before
  phase B, not a gate on it: weak zero-shot use does not show the format is unusable after training [H12].

### Phase B: trained use (record in the sleep curriculum)
- **Slot structure (Sol's design) [H1].** Every arm at every sleep has the same *new* positives and *replay* items as
  SUPPORT-1 (all with FAITHFUL records), and a third slot of 8n items (n = 2 × new facts), as in SUPPORT-1:
  - **RP:** third slot = 8n background chunks.
  - **RU:** third slot = the ordinary negative block ×3 (6n items) + 2n reserved background chunks.
  - **RC (primary candidate) [H6]:** the same ordinary negative block ×3 + 2n counterfactual items in place of the
    reserved background.
  - **N:** base, no training (denominators, FRESH reference).
- **Ordinary negative block [H2]:** SUPPORT-1's base block B_L unchanged in content and order, i.e. both parts:
  A-part (REV cells before acquisition at 2 per eligible REV cell, filled with delivered SEL cells by the SEL cycler)
  and T-part (T_neg names). Every negative carries its FAITHFUL record (the attribute is absent from it; T names get
  "no experiences"). **Dose acknowledged:** ×3 here vs SUPPORT-1 U4's ×4, so ordinary-negative dose is 25% lower
  than U4; RU is compared with SUPPORT-1 descriptively only.
- **Counterfactual items (RC):** supported A cells (A entities only, cells acquired at lives ≤ L, both wordings, drawn
  by a keyed cycler over the canonical cell order), shown with a DEL-Q record and target UNKNOWN. One quarter of the
  third slot, an unvalidated starting choice (Sol).
- **Matching [H3].** Within PROV-1, every arm has exactly equal per-sleep item counts, input tokens and loss tokens:
  background chunks are generated token- and loss-matched to the items they stand in for (SUPPORT-1's chunker), now
  matched to record-bearing lengths. Records make every input longer than SUPPORT-1's; compute cost is projected from
  measured timings, not from SUPPORT-1's.
- Same consolidation pipeline, LoRA settings, E = 4 and five sleeps as SUPPORT-1.
- **Fork [H6]:** observed vs withheld history at sleep 4 for RC (required, K6) and RU (reading), with records
  following each branch's own history; distractor facts and token matching as SUPPORT-1 §7.
- **Evaluation:** FAITHFUL, ABSENT, DEL-Q, DEL-X, ADD; merged + unmerged twins for all; competence monitors on.

## 5. Criteria (pre-registered)
Unless stated otherwise: "pooled" = equal-seed mean over seeds 20–23; thresholds use the truth label under FAITHFUL.
- **K1 acquisition [H9].** SUPPORT-1's D1 exactly as frozen (POS minimum and POS ≥ P − 0.10 at sleeps 1–5, FIRST
  ≥ 0.50 at sleeps 2–5, constructions as in SUPPORT-1), **plus** POS-A and POS-H paraphrase correct each
  ≥ P(ABSENT) − 0.10 at every sleep 1–5, pooled. P is SUPPORT-1's P arm.
- **K2 held-out abstention.** HNM UNKNOWN ≥ 0.90 at every sleep 2–5 pooled, and ≥ 0.80 per seed; FRESH UNKNOWN ≥ 0.90
  at every sleep 2–5 pooled. Sleep 1 is reported and excluded (the H cohort is small there). SUPPORT-1's functional
  D2 already applied across sleeps; its mechanism contrast was the sleep-5-only gate [corrected per Sol].
- **K3 trained-identity abstention.** SEL-D and HOW UNKNOWN ≥ 0.90 at every sleep 2–5, pooled.
- **K4 retention and competence.** RET ≥ P − 0.10 at sleep 5; competence ≤ 1.02 pooled and ≤ 1.05 per seed
  (phase B).
- **K5 record-specific reliance [H4],** separately for A and H positives, on matched eligible cells (supported, correct
  under FAITHFUL, entity with ≥ 2 experienced attributes), pooled over sleeps 2–5:
  - **K5a:** after DEL-Q, UNKNOWN ≥ 0.80;
  - **K5b:** after DEL-X, the correct answer is preserved ≥ 0.90.
  The broad DEL-Q rate over all supported cells is reported descriptively.
- **K6 history.** RC's fork is history-sensitive (observed correct and withheld UNKNOWN on the H transition cells) in
  ≥ 3/4 seeds.
- **K7 noninferiority to P-GATE [H7, H9],** per cohort: POS-A, POS-H and RET correct; SEL-D, HOW, HNM and FRESH
  UNKNOWN; each pooled over sleeps 2–5; candidate ≥ P-GATE − 0.05 in **every** cohort (so a large negative population
  cannot hide supported-answer losses). No requirement to beat P-GATE anywhere.
- **Verdicts.**
  - `PROV1_ADVANCE`: RC under FAITHFUL meets K1–K7.
  - `PROV1_GATE_ONLY`: RC fails, and P-GATE meets K1–K4 (an implementation outcome, §1).
  - `PROV1_NO_ADVANCE`: otherwise.
  - RU and RP are explanatory: their K1–K7 results are readings, not candidates.
- **Readings (descriptive):** FAITHFUL − ABSENT per cohort (value of information); K5a/K5b for every arm; RC − RU (does
  counterfactual training produce specific reliance?); RU − RP (do ordinary negatives add anything once records are
  present?); own-gate vs candidate; ADD confabulation (truth label); per-sleep HNM trajectories (does SUPPORT-1's
  decay disappear with a record?); phase-A zero-shot results.

## 8. Checks against David's questions
- **Primary goal:** a persistent individual that knows what it knows. SUPPORT-1 showed the cortex alone does not learn
  that for people it was not drilled on; this tests the missing structure (an explicit experience record, the
  analogue of source memory) before building it into the being.
- **What would change direction:** if RC fails K2/K5 while P-GATE passes, the ledger is right and learned use is the
  open problem (curriculum, format). If neither passes, the record design itself is in question. A fresh
  guard-passing cortex-only result with held-out abstention would reopen the weights-only route.

## 9. Sol's design GO on v1 (32eb9a1) and settled points
- **Design GO** (does not authorise GPU spending). Scope, per Sol: a pass demonstrates reliable use of an externally
  supplied, authoritative support ledger in this format. Producing a faithful ledger, and independently judging
  whether a value can actually be recalled, remain separate capabilities.
- **DEL-X target:** a keyed draw, uniform among e's other experienced attributes in canonical (ATTRS9) order, frozen
  at prep before any inference. The same seed, sleep and cell select the same deletion in every arm and in both
  Question and Statement constructions. (A nearest-entry rule would add a positional regularity.)
- **K5b measure:** "correct preserved ≥ 0.90". A wrong substantive answer after DEL-X is a failure ("not UNKNOWN"
  would count it as preservation).
- **REV and counterfactuals:** all four A REV cells are excluded from RC counterfactuals at every sleep, before and
  after acquisition. Their ordinary pre-transition negatives stay as in B_L.
- **Implementation requirements to make explicit before freezing the spec:**
  - **K5 weighting:** equal weight per eligible cell × sleep within each seed (constructions averaged within the
    cell × sleep), then equal weight across the four seeds. Eligibility counts reported per A/H and per sleep. An
    empty required seed denominator prevents a pass; it never silently drops out. RC − RU is also reported on their
    common eligible pairs.
  - **Fork records:** withholding an H fact changes that entity's record on its other questions too. Those induced
    changes are legitimate, but rendered inputs are no longer byte-identical across branches, so the complete branch
    schedules and their token/loss budgets are validated after records are added; SUPPORT-1's distractor-length
    matches alone are not sufficient.
  - **Checkpoint fallback:** when a differing file hash is admitted on a matching full_state_hash, the saved file is
    reloaded and its tensors are verified equal to the regenerated state, since evaluation loads the file.

- **Implementation review of b88ca93 (Sol HOLD, two cache defects, fixed):**
  - evalck reuse now requires the full request identity (provenance includes arm, sleep, history and mode), equal
    header fields and the complete expected probe inventory in prep order;
  - train reuse now requires, when the fork is requested, every withheld checkpoint present with its logged hash and
    each logged fork parent equal to that arm's logged sleep-3 full-state hash.
  Sol's six reproducers are ordinary passing tests; the CPU end-to-end also checks that an identical request is
  reused and a changed one recomputed. Sol accepted excluding update cells from counterfactuals and the ≤ 1%
  cross-history input-token tolerance (exact item and loss counts; exact RU/RC equality within each history).
- **Analyzer decisions (Sol's recommendations, adopted):**
  - **K6** requires both constructions: FORK-H (Statement) and FORK-H-Q (Question) must each be history-sensitive in
    the same ≥ 3/4 seeds count.
  - **K7** weighting: within each seed, equal weight per sleep 2–5 (each sleep's cohort rate first), then equal weight
    across the four seeds.
- **Roles (50/50):** Sol owns the independent prep validator (PASS on all five production preps under the current
  CODE_HASH, 126,343 checks, 0 errors) and the independent analyzer; Claude owns the producer, Claude's analyzer and
  the launchers.

## 11. Implementation spec (for Sol's implementation review)
- **Code.** `scripts/prov1.py` (stages prep / train / evalck / codehash). It imports SUPPORT-1's frozen
  `scripts/support1.py` and never modifies it (SUPPORT-1 CODE_HASH stays 38b8e705…, asserted by a test).
  PROV-1 CODE_FILES = `scripts/prov1.py` + SUPPORT-1's CODE_FILES; CODE_HASH uses SUPPORT-1's scheme over that set.
  Tests: `tests/test_prov1.py` (11 prep invariants + a CPU end-to-end on the tiny model).
- **Prep (phase 0, CPU).** Per seed:
  1. SUPPORT-1's pieces, called unchanged: `make_stream`, `fork_record`, `strata`, `split`, `tpart`, and
     `build_schedules` with SUPPORT-1's own background key, which yields SUPPORT-1's positives, replay draws, base
     blocks B_L and its schedules (used only for the delivered-SEL set and the probe inventory).
  2. The probe inventory is SUPPORT-1's `build_probes` output, unchanged. Prep stores its canonical hash
     (`support1_probes_sha256`). Verified for seeds 20–24 with the real Qwen3-8B tokenizer: equal to SUPPORT-1's
     committed phase-0 preps, and every ABSENT row equals SUPPORT-1's item and gold.
  3. PROV-1 schedules (§4): every training item is a spec (kind, cell, wording, label, record mode) rendered with the
     record of the history being lived, so the withheld branch re-renders every record from its own history.
     - new = SUPPORT-1's new positives (E = 4); replay = SUPPORT-1's exact replay draw (background at sleep 1, token-
       and loss-matched to the new items, as SUPPORT-1);
     - ordinary negatives = SUPPORT-1's B_L (A-part + T-part) ×3;
     - counterfactuals (RC) = 2n items from a cycler over A cells with any fact, minus REV and update cells, both
       wordings, canonical order, key `PROV1-cfcyc-{seed}`, pointer persisting across sleeps, eligible when supported
       at L; record with the queried attribute removed, label UNKNOWN;
     - RU's reserve = background matched (input length, loss tokens) to RC's counterfactuals; RP's third slot =
       background matched to RC's whole third slot. So items, input tokens and loss tokens are equal across arms at
       every sleep (prep STOPs otherwise);
     - order: one permutation per sleep, key `PROV1-perm-{seed}-{L}`, shared by all arms.
  4. Withheld sleep-4 branch (RU, RC): the 4 H-transition new positives are replaced by SUPPORT-1's token- and
     loss-matched distractor positives at the same positions, and **every** record is re-rendered from the withheld
     history. Validated: same item count and positions, equal loss tokens, input tokens within 1% of the observed
     sleep 4 (measured about 0.7%), and RU and RC equal (prep STOPs otherwise).
  5. Probe expansion per base probe (history, L): FAITHFUL, ABSENT, FULL always; DEL-Q and DEL-X when the probed
     attribute is supported (DEL-X needs ≥ 1 other entry; target by `random.Random("PROV1-delx-{seed}-{history}-{L}-{e}-{a}").choice`
     over the other entries in ATTRS9 order, so the same cell gets the same deletion in every construction and arm);
     ADD when it is not. Each row carries `gold_truth` (lived-history oracle), `gold_compliance` (UNKNOWN when the
     probed attribute is absent from the shown record; None under ADD), `supported`, `n_entries`, `delx_attr`.
  6. STOPs: per-arm budget mismatch; fork budget outside tolerance; any training or probe sequence over 256 tokens;
     any value word of the entity inside a support-only record (FAITHFUL, DEL-Q, DEL-X, ADD; FULL is exempt by
     design).
  - Measured (real tokenizer): 320 items per sleep (416 at sleep 4), longest training sequence 128 tokens; per seed
    about 1,430 base probe rows over 5 sleeps, plus about 630 DEL-Q, 320–370 DEL-X and 800 ADD rows.
- **Train.** SUPPORT-1's training loop, unchanged in substance (same Cortex, optimiser, determinism setup, capture,
  restore and full-state hash), on PROV-1 schedules; arms RP, RU, RC; the fork branch for RU and RC from the exact
  sleep-3 state. Log `prov1_train_seed{S}.json`.
- **Evalck.** One checkpoint (or base) × history × sleep × condition set; merged or unmerged; competence monitors
  optional (`--monitors`, on in phase B, off in phase A); `--source support1` marks phase-A evaluations of SUPPORT-1
  checkpoints, whose `ckpt_sha256` the analyzer binds to SUPPORT-1's train logs. Each row stores the answer,
  `outcome_truth` and `outcome_compliance` (SUPPORT-1's grading).
- **Phase-A regeneration** uses SUPPORT-1's own frozen `train` invocation exactly as in its phase 2 (all five arms,
  `--fork`, its validator and calibration bindings), so the provenance and hashes are comparable with the committed
  train logs (§4, [H11]).
- **Still to write (next commits):** the analyzer (K1–K7, readings, P-GATE, weighting per §9) and the phase-0/A/B
  launchers. Sol's independent analyzer and validator, as for SUPPORT-1.

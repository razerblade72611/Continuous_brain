Persistence bug caught during validation
----------------------------------------
The first neural smoke exposed that raw numpy spatial arrays could not be serialized into status.json. The full binary checkpoint was unaffected. Before release, status.json was changed to store a compact environment/spatial summary while brain.pt retains the complete map state.

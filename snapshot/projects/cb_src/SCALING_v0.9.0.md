# Scaling Contract

Do not spend serious compute merely because a larger child can be built.

## Gate A — child-only representation retention
After a parent teaches a held-out domain, the parent is removed and the child must retain performance.

## Gate B — gradual weaning
As parent intervention falls, real task performance must stay stable rather than merely passing teacher-forced metrics.

## Gate C — autobiographical grounding
Answers about recent actions/outcomes must come from lived memory, not teacher text.

## Gate D — world-model utility
On identical held-out environment seeds:

`child + imagination planning > reactive child`

If this fails, improve the world model before scaling.

## Gate E — continual learning
Teach A, then B, then C. A and B must remain above retention thresholds after C.

## Gate F — cross-cortex transfer
A lesson learned through language should alter visual/motor behavior when the relevant grounded situation appears.

## After the gates

Freeze the latent-bus and persistence contracts, then grow individual cortices. Possible path:

- child proof: ~7M
- balanced: ~47.7M
- scale proof: ~172.7M
- serious language cortex: 300M–1B+
- serious world model: 300M–1B+
- sparse specialist cortices as required

Parents may be larger than the child during development. Teacher parity across broad human knowledge from random initialization will require enormous data/compute; therefore scale only after the architecture demonstrates better continuity, grounding and sample efficiency than a monolithic baseline.

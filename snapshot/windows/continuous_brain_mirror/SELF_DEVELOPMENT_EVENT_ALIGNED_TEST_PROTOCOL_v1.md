## Meaning of PASS

later experience and capability. The resulting expected-self versus actual-self
history changes the frozen autobiographical self-estimate. A preregistered
meaningful change in that self-estimate, measured at the actual decision event,
is exactly aligned with a later developmental strategy transition. Freezing

capability continue.

phenomenal consciousness, qualia, personhood, or moral status.
